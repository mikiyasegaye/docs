# Ethio Clock Privacy Policy

*Effective 17 October 2026 · App package `com.ethioclock.app`*

Ethio Clock shows Ethiopian time, the Ethiopian calendar and Ethiopian Orthodox feasts in home-screen widgets. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing leaves your phone.

## Information we collect

None. Ethio Clock does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

## Information kept on your phone

The app saves your settings (theme, appearance, accent colour, hour system and which month each calendar widget shows) on your phone so your widgets look the way you chose. These settings never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for what its widgets need: to schedule a once-a-minute update so the clocks stay current, and to restart that update after your phone restarts. Neither gives the app access to your personal data.

## Children

Ethio Clock collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
