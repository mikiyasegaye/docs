# ዜማ መቃኛ (Zema Tuner) Privacy Policy

*Effective 17 October 2026 · App package `com.orthodox.zema_mekagna`*

ዜማ መቃኛ tunes the instruments of Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo mezmur (begena, kirar, masinko and washint) to their kignet, detects which kignet an instrument is tuned to, and includes a metronome and tap tempo. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing leaves your phone.

## Information we collect

None. ዜማ መቃኛ does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

## The microphone

To hear your instrument, the app listens through the microphone while the tuner or kignet detection is on screen. The sound is analysed on your phone, moment by moment, to find its pitch, and is then discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app stops listening as soon as you leave those screens or the app.

## Information kept on your phone

The app saves your settings (instrument, kignet, key, note names, language, reference pitch, in-tune range, microphone sensitivity, reference tone, theme, and the metronome's tempo and trainer options) on your phone so the app opens the way you left it. These settings never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for the microphone, for the reason above. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time; the tuner and kignet detection will then ask for it again, while the metronome and tap tempo keep working without it.

## Updates

When you open the app, it asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version of ዜማ መቃኛ is available, and if so Google Play offers to update it. That check is made by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy; ዜማ መቃኛ itself sends nothing.

## Children

ዜማ መቃኛ collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
